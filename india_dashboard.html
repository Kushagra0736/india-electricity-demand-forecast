Methodology & data

Data. Grid-India (NLDC) Monthly Operational Reports, . Demand is energy met (MU). Credit: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.

Method. Average of (a) same month last year × recent year-on-year growth and (b) a gradient-boosting model of that growth. Ranges are the 80th-percentile backtest error at each horizon.

What-if. For Apr–Sep, +1 °C vs last year went with about % more demand across states (95% interval ). No effect was detectable Oct–Mar.

Limits. About 4 years of monthly data; small states are noisy; beyond ~5 months ahead the model is no better than last year × growth. Not an official forecast.
